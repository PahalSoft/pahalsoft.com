Delete your account and data

Wheel of Words · PahalSoft · Last updated: August 2026

You can delete your Wheel of Words account and everything stored with it at any time, from inside the app. Nothing is kept afterwards, and you do not need to contact us first.

How to delete it

  1. Open Wheel of Words.
  2. Go to the Profile tab, then tap the settings icon.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

The game is playable without an account, so most players are signed in anonymously and have no username or password. That is why deletion is done from inside the app, where you are already signed in — it is the only way we can be certain the request comes from the person the data belongs to.

What is deleted

  • Your account itself.
  • Your synced progress: words learned, levels completed, streak, coins and gems.
  • Your display name and profile picture, and your leaderboard entry if you had one.
  • Your subscription record on our side.
  • Everything the app has saved on your device, including settings.

This is immediate and cannot be undone. There is no recovery window and no backup we can restore from.

What is not deleted, and why

An active subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or the App Store, not by us, and only the store can cancel one. If you have Odyssey Pro, cancel it in your store account as well — otherwise you will continue to be charged for a game you can no longer open. The app reminds you of this before it deletes anything.

We may retain records we are legally required to keep, such as purchase and tax records held by the app stores. These contain no gameplay data and are not linked to your progress.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Uninstalling removes everything stored on your device. If you also want data removed from our servers and can no longer open the app, email us and we will delete it manually. Tell us the display name you used, if you set one, so we can find the right account.

Kids Mode

Kids Mode has no account and stores nothing on our servers — there is nothing to delete beyond the app itself. Uninstalling removes everything. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
