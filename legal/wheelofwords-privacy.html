Privacy Policy

Wheel of Words · Last updated: August 2026

Wheel of Words is made by PahalSoft (“we”, “our”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and how that changes with the age of the person playing.

1. The short version

The game is playable with no account and no internet connection, and your progress is stored on your own device. What the app is allowed to collect depends on which mode it is running in, and that is decided once, by age, when you first open it.

2. Age and modes

On first launch the app asks for a date of birth. It is used to choose the right mode and is stored on the device only — it is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

  • Kids Mode (under 13) — no account, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising, no push notifications, no purchases, and no links out to the web.
  • Teen Mode (13–17) — adds an optional account and cloud backup of progress. No analytics, no advertising, no push notifications.
  • Standard Mode (18+) — the full app, including analytics and optional reminders.

These are not settings that filter features at runtime. The components that would collect data are not created at all in the modes that do not permit them.

3. What is stored on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, streak, the words you have met and how well you know them, and your settings are all stored locally on your device. In Kids Mode this is the only place any of it exists.

4. Accounts and cloud backup

In Teen and Standard Mode the app can create an anonymous account so your progress can be restored on a new device. Anonymous means exactly that: it is an identifier with no name, email or phone number attached. If you later choose to sign in with Google, Apple or email, that identity is linked to the same progress. You can play the entire game without ever signing in.

Synced data covers progress only — levels completed, word history, settings. We never upload the contents of your device, your contacts, your location or your messages.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

Analytics runs in Standard Mode only. It records anonymous events about how the game is used — levels started and finished, features opened — so we can see what works. It is switched off at the operating-system level in Kids and Teen Mode, before the app finishes starting.

Crash reporting runs in every mode, because a crash in a child’s game is still worth fixing. Outside Standard Mode it is anonymised: no user identifier is attached and no custom context is recorded. A crash report contains technical information about the failure — device model, operating system version, and where in the code the error happened.

6. Advertising

Wheel of Words contains no advertising. There is no advertising software in the app, and it does not request or use the advertising identifier on your device.

If we ever introduce advertising, it would be in Standard Mode only — never to children or teenagers — and we would update this policy and our Google Play declaration before any such version was released.

7. Notifications

Optional, Standard Mode only, and never requested at first launch. Kids and Teen Mode do not register for notifications at all, because a notification token is a persistent device identifier.

8. Service providers

Where the app does use the network, it relies on Google Firebase for accounts, cloud backup, crash reporting and — in Standard Mode — analytics and messaging. The app currently offers nothing to buy; if paid items are added, payment would be handled by Apple or Google and we would never see your card details.

9. Children’s privacy

Kids Mode is designed so there is nothing to collect: no account, no network sync of personal data, no analytics and no purchase surface. The app contains no advertising in any mode. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Settings that change how the app behaves are placed behind a parental gate in Kids Mode.

10. Your choices

  • Play without an account — available in every mode.
  • Delete your data by deleting the app, which removes everything stored locally.
  • Request deletion of any synced data by emailing us.
  • Turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Any update will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
