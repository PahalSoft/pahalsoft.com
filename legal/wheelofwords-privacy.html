Privacy Policy

Wheel of Words · Last updated: 12 August 2026

Wheel of Words is made by PahalSoft (“we”, “our”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and how that changes with the age of the person playing.

1. The short version

The game is playable with no account and no internet connection, and your progress is stored on your own device. What the app is allowed to collect depends on which mode it is running in, and that is decided once, by age, when you first open it.

2. Age and modes

On first launch the app asks for a date of birth. It is used to choose the right mode and is stored on the device only — it is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

  • Kids Mode — under 13, anywhere. No account, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising, no notifications, no purchases, and no links out to the web.
  • Teen Mode — 13 to 15 in the EEA and UK. Adds an optional account, cloud backup of progress, and optional purchases. No analytics, no advertising, no notifications.
  • Standard Mode — 13 and over elsewhere, 16 and over in the EEA and UK. The full app: analytics, advertising, optional purchases, an optional leaderboard and an optional daily reminder.

These are not settings that filter features at runtime. The components that would collect data are not created at all in the modes that do not permit them.

3. What is stored on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, streak, the words you have met and how well you know them, and your settings are all stored locally on your device. In Kids Mode this is the only place any of it exists.

4. Accounts and cloud backup

In Teen and Standard Mode the app can create an anonymous account so your progress can be restored on a new device. Anonymous means exactly that: it is an identifier with no name, email or phone number attached. If you later choose to sign in with Google, that identity is linked to the same progress. Google is currently the only sign-in offered. You can play the entire game without ever signing in.

Synced data covers progress only — levels completed, word history, settings. We never upload the contents of your device, your contacts, your location or your messages.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

Analytics runs in Standard Mode only. It records anonymous events about how the game is used — levels started and finished, features opened — so we can see what works. It is switched off at the operating-system level in Kids and Teen Mode, before the app finishes starting.

Crash reporting runs in every mode, because a crash in a child’s game is still worth fixing. Outside Standard Mode it is anonymised: no user identifier is attached and no custom context is recorded. A crash report contains technical information about the failure — device model, operating system version, and where in the code the error happened.

6. Advertising

Advertising runs in Standard Mode only — never to children or teenagers. In Kids and Teen Mode the advertising software is not started at all, so there is nothing to disable and nothing that could show an ad.

Where ads do appear they are supplied by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between levels, and an optional video you can choose to watch in exchange for in-game coins. You are never required to watch one.

The ads are not personalised. The app does not request or use the advertising identifier on your device — the permission is removed from the app entirely — and every ad request is explicitly marked non-personalised. You will see ads, but they are not selected using a profile of you.

In the EEA and the UK you are asked for your advertising choices before any advertising software starts, through Google's consent form. If you have not answered, no ads are requested.

7. Notifications

Optional, Standard Mode only, and never requested at first launch — the app asks only after you have finished a Daily Challenge.

The daily reminder is scheduled by your own device. Nothing about it leaves your phone, no messaging token is requested, and it works with no internet connection. You can turn it off in the app, or in your device settings, at any time. Kids and Teen Mode schedule nothing at all.

8. Leaderboard

Standard Mode has an optional leaderboard, and it is off unless you switch it on. Switching it on publishes your chosen display name and picture, and the number of words you have mastered, to other players. Nothing else about you is published, and turning it off removes you from the board. Kids and Teen Mode have no leaderboard.

9. Service providers

Where the app does use the network, it relies on Google Firebase for accounts, cloud backup and crash reporting, and — in Standard Mode only — Google Analytics for Firebase and Google AdMob.

Purchases are available in Teen and Standard Mode. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see your card details. RevenueCat processes the purchase receipt on our behalf so the app knows what you are entitled to.

10. Children’s privacy

Kids Mode is designed so there is nothing to collect: no account, no network sync of personal data, no analytics, no advertising and no purchase surface. None of that software is started in a child's session, so it is absent rather than switched off. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Settings that change how the app behaves are placed behind a parental gate in Kids Mode.

Parents and guardians: our Children's Privacy Notice says the same thing in plain language, without the rest of this document around it.

11. Your choices

  • Play without an account — available in every mode.
  • Delete your account and all of its data from inside the app, under Settings → Account. See how to delete your account.
  • Delete the app, which removes everything stored locally on your device.
  • Turn the daily reminder off in the app, or in your device settings, at any time.
  • Stay off the leaderboard, which is where it is unless you switch it on.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Any update will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
