2. What the app does

App Locker asks for a PIN or a fingerprint before opening applications you have chosen to protect. It reads only the identifier of the application being opened — not its contents.

3. What the app does not protect against

App Locker is designed to deter casual access — someone picking up your unlocked phone, or being handed it. It is not protection against a determined person with time, technical knowledge and physical access to your device.

  • Anyone who can reach Android's own settings can disable App Locker.
  • Anyone who can uninstall applications can remove it.

  • It is not a substitute for your device's own lock screen, which you should also use.
  • It cannot protect data extracted from the device by other means.

Please do not rely on App Locker as your only safeguard for information whose disclosure would seriously harm you.

If you forget your PIN, it cannot be recovered — by you, by us, or by anyone else. There is no reset link, because there is no account and no server. If that happens, the remedy is to uninstall App Locker, which removes your settings and unlocks your applications. Your applications and their data are unaffected.
