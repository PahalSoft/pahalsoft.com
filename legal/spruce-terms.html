3. What the app does

Spruce measures and, when you confirm, removes files and software on an Ubuntu computer: caches and logs, files you choose, apps you choose to uninstall, and old software versions. It can install updates, change some system settings you ask it to change, and scan for viruses with ClamAV. It does none of these things without showing you what will happen and waiting for your confirmation.

4. Check before you confirm

This section matters more than most, so it is stated plainly rather than buried.

Spruce is designed to be careful: your own files go to the Trash rather than being deleted outright, one copy of every duplicate is always kept, and parts of Ubuntu that the system needs cannot be removed. But some actions cannot be undone:

  • Emptying the Trash, and deleting a file from quarantine, are permanent.
  • Cleared caches, logs and old software versions are removed, not moved to the Trash.
  • Uninstalling an app removes it and, if you tick them, its leftover folders.
  • Installed updates replace the previous versions of the software they update.

Please read what each confirmation lists, and keep backups of anything you could not stand to lose. You are responsible for what you choose to remove.
