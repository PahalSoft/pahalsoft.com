Privacy Policy

Timisha · Last updated: 25 August 2026

Timisha is made by PahalSoft (“we”, “our”, “us”). This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, and where the one genuine network connection goes.

1. The short version

Your photographs never leave your device. They are decoded, edited and exported on the phone itself, using its own graphics hardware. There is no upload step, no account, and no server holding your images. The app does make one kind of network connection, and it is not about your photos — see section 5.

2. What is stored on your device

All of the following stays in Timisha's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • Your edits — the adjustments, masks and crop for each photo, stored as instructions rather than as pixels.
  • A reference to the photo — the system identifier of the file you opened, not a copy of it.
  • Your saved presets — any looks you chose to save.
  • Your settings — export defaults, and whether the introduction has been seen.
  • Whether you have bought Pro — a single yes or no.

Your original photo file is never modified. Edits are kept separately and applied when you ask for an export, which writes a new file. Deleting Timisha removes the edits and leaves every original exactly as it was.

3. What is not stored, anywhere

  • No copies of your photographs on any server.
  • No name, email address or account of any kind.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No location data collected by us. Photos may carry location in their own EXIF data; see section 6.
  • No analytics, usage tracking or behavioural profiling by us.

4. Photo library access

Timisha asks for access to your photos so it can show your library and open the picture you choose. You can decline and still use the app: the “choose a single photo” option hands over one image at a time through Android's own picker, and the app never sees the rest of your library.

Access can be withdrawn at any time in Android Settings. Doing so leaves your saved edits intact but means Timisha can no longer open the photos they refer to.

5. The internet permission, and what uses it

Timisha holds Android's INTERNET permission. We would rather explain it here than have you discover it in the app listing and draw your own conclusion.

It is required by Google Play Billing, which is how a Pro purchase is verified, restored on a new device, and refunded if you ask Google for a refund. That exchange is between your device and Google. It carries purchase information, not image data. Google's handling of it is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The billing library also brings in Google's own diagnostics component, which may report on the billing library's operation to Google. We do not receive that information, and it does not contain your photographs.

No part of Timisha sends image data anywhere. Not the editor, not the export, and not the automatic subject selection described below.

6. Location in exported files

Photographs often record where they were taken. When Timisha exports a copy, it removes location by default, so sharing an edited photo does not disclose where you were. Other camera information — the model, exposure and date — is carried across so the file remains useful.

You can change this in Settings if you want location preserved. It is off by default because the safer choice should not depend on finding a setting.

7. Automatic subject selection

Timisha can find the subject of a photograph so you can adjust it separately. This runs on your device, using a machine-learning model included inside the app. The photograph is not sent anywhere for this, and no network connection is made in order to do it.

8. Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card details, billing address, or Google account. What Timisha stores is a single flag recording that the purchase exists.

9. Data sharing

We share nothing, because we hold nothing. There is no analytics provider, no advertising network, no cloud storage and no crash reporting service. The only third party involved at all is Google, in its role as the payment processor for the store you installed the app from.

10. Children

Timisha is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what leaves your device, this policy will be updated and the change will be stated plainly in the app before it takes effect — not disclosed quietly in a document nobody is prompted to reread.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@pahalsoft.com.
