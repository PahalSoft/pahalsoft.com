Website Privacy

pahalsoft.com · Last updated: 28 November 2026

This page is about this website, pahalsoft.com. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked from its page; this one changes nothing in them.

1. The short version

If you agree, we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people find useful. If you decline, or don't choose, no analytics cookies are set. We show no ads, and we never use this to identify you.

2. What is collected, if you agree

  • Which pages you visit and how you got here (for example a search engine or a link).
  • Your approximate location (country and city), device type, browser and screen size.
  • When you visit and roughly how long you stay.

Google Analytics does not store your IP address. We don't send it your name, email or anything you type on this site.

3. Cookies

With your consent, Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<ID>, which tell repeat visits apart. Your choice itself is remembered in your browser's local storage (ps-consent) so we don't ask again. Google Tag Manager, which loads Analytics, sets no cookies of its own.

4. Who processes it, and for how long

Google LLC processes the analytics data for us, under Google's privacy policy, and keeps it for up to 14 months. We only look at totals: visits per page, countries, devices and referrers.

5. Your choice

You can change your mind at any time. Declining stops new analytics data; you can also delete this site's cookies in your browser settings.
