Review you never see

No flashcards, no drills. The next level is simply chosen to contain the words you are closest to forgetting.

Every word means something

Definitions, pronunciation and an example sentence for the words you meet — not just letters that happen to fit.

Chapters and stages

Ten levels to a stage, so there is always something you can finish in one sitting.

Yours, on your device

Progress is stored locally and the game works with no signal at all. Signing in is optional.

Puzzles you enjoy. Vocabulary that stays.

Most word games are a pleasant way to pass time and nothing is left afterwards. Shabd Chakra keeps a record of every word you have met and how well you know it, then uses a proven spaced-repetition schedule to decide what to put in front of you next.

My Words

Every word you have met, with how close it is to mastered. Search it, read the definition, hear how it sounds, and see when the game plans to test you again. Mastery means four correct recalls with the gaps stretched past three weeks — not four right answers in a row on the same afternoon.

Built so a child can play it safely.

Shabd Chakra asks for a date of birth once, on the device, and never sends it anywhere. Under 13, the game runs in Kids Mode: no account, no cloud, no analytics, no advertising, no notifications, and no way out to the web. Those features are not hidden behind a switch — they are not built into that version of the app at all.
