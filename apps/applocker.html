Privacy, on your own device

Hand over your phone. Not everything on it.

App Locker puts a PIN or fingerprint in front of the apps you would rather other people did not open. It works entirely offline — not as a setting you switch on, but because the app has no permission to reach the internet at all.

Offline is not a feature. It is the architecture.

Plenty of apps promise they do not collect your data. App Locker is built so that it could not, even if it wanted to: it does not request Android's internet permission, and an app without that permission cannot open a network connection. Android enforces that, not us.

Check it yourself

You do not have to take any of this on trust. Open Android Settings → Apps → App Locker → Permissions and you will find one entry: biometric access. No internet, no storage, no contacts, no camera. Look under Data usage and App Locker will not appear at all, because it has never made a connection.

About that Accessibility permission.

App Locker needs Android's Accessibility Service, and Android will show you a strongly worded warning when you enable it. That warning is fair: the API can be used to read everything on screen.

App Locker uses it for one thing. When an app comes to the foreground, the service reads that app's identifier — something like com.example.messaging — and nothing else. No screen contents, no text, no notifications, nothing you type. And with no internet permission, anything it did read would have nowhere to go.

What this protects against — and what it does not

App Locker is built for an ordinary situation: someone picks up your unlocked phone, or you hand it to them, and you would rather they did not open certain apps. It is good at that.

It is not protection against someone with time, skill and physical access to your device. Anyone who reaches Android's own settings can switch App Locker off, and anyone who can uninstall apps can remove it. It does not encrypt your files, and it does not replace your device's lock screen. An app in this category that claims otherwise is not being straight with you.
